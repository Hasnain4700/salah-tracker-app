Account Deletion Request

At Salah Tracker, we respect your privacy and provide you with full control over your data. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, please follow the steps below:

How to Request Deletion:

You can request account deletion by sending an email from your registered email address to:

support@salahtracker.com

What Data Will Be Deleted:

  • Your personal profile information (Email, Display Name).
  • Your prayer logs and historical data.
  • Your XP, rewards, and "Jannat ka Darakht" progress.
  • Social connections (Deen Twins, Halaqa Circle memberships).
  • Your FCM push notification tokens.

Note: Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered. We usually process deletion requests within 7 business days.
